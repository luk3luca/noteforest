Page — a scroll of pages

One column, page wide, free scroll. Pages touch and a 1 px rule is the seam. No snapping, no spread, no page badge in the gap: the page number lives in the toolbar indicator, which tracks the page under the centre of the viewport. Fit width is the rest state; zoom in is a reading aid, not a mode.

1 · Scrolled to the seam

This is the case that matters: the bottom half of one page and the top half of the next, with the seam between them. The indicator reads the page whose area contains the centre of the viewport. Try scrolling inside the frame.

The indicator flips when the seam crosses the vertical centre of the frame — not when a page becomes fully visible.

2 · The zoom states

Fit width is the rest state. Zooming in makes the page wider than the viewport, so a horizontal scrollbar appears — a temporary magnifier for a compact density. Zooming out only ever adds margins: one column, never two pages side by side.

3 · The rules

RuleValue
ColumnOne page wide, never two. Works for portrait and landscape.
ScrollFree, no snapping. Half a page above and below is a normal state.
SeamPages touch, 1 px --paper-rule line. No gap, no shadow, no number.
Indicator3 / 61 in the toolbar, the page under the viewport centre; a tie keeps the current page.
ZoomFit width is rest; zoom in shows a horizontal scrollbar; zoom out adds margins.
EdgesTrunks stop at the seam and resume on the next page. The rule is the cut.
MountingVisible pages ±2; the rest are placeholders of the page size, so the scrollbar stays truthful.

What this fixes. A manual break becomes visible in its cause: you see the blank tail it leaves on the previous page and the shift it causes below, without pressing anything.

What it is not. Not a change to paginate, the page model, the page chrome or the PDF; not a two-page spread; not a change to Continuous, which stays the canvas with arrows and no page chrome.

Lab only, no application code. Paper is the app's light Page surface. Page heights are schematic: the real one is A4 at the effective theme, from the shared page model.
