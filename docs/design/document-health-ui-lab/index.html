Health lives in the sidebar

The sidebar is the report: grouped sections, expandable, every error one click away. The repair opens in the sidebar itself — the column widens by 400 px and the detail appears to the right of the list. Nothing is duplicated and nothing shifts. No left stripes anywhere: a state is an icon and a word, not a bar.

why it matters

The repair needs room for a quoted candidate, a before/after and the branch list. Inside 264 px those become accordions inside accordions, and the list of other issues disappears exactly when you need it. Keep the sidebar for finding, the widened column for deciding.

Geometry

Section row 30 px · error row 27 px · control 28 px · panel 400 px next to the 264 px sidebar. Radius 4 px on rows and controls, 8 px on the panel. No severity stripe: an icon plus a word in the detail, the section name in the list.
