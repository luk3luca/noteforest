Health: sidebar report, modal repair

The sidebar holds the report: expandable sections, one row per issue. Clicking an issue opens the repair in a modal in front. Confirm closes it and the sidebar updates — there is nothing to reopen, because the report was never hidden. This lab shows the refresh placement, section vs row, the 12 health plans and one modal language before/after for every dialog shape.

  • Sidebar is the reportSections expand; the error row is the selection.
  • Click opens the modalOne issue, its context, its choices.
  • Confirm closesThe row leaves the list, Undo in the toast. No next step.

264 px · same content

The modal, simplified

the four you sent · before / after

You are right: these read as documentation, not as a fix. The same information is repeated, internal data leaks in, and every issue invents its own sections. The proposal is one skeleton with three blocks at most: where you are, what is wrong, what to choose.

The rules

1 · Context: the quote and the block, never twice. 2 · Problem: one sentence. 3 · Decision: candidates or a target select, only when needed. Branches are one line, only when they exist. No “Stale bindings”, no node paths, no anchor IDs, no “Text anchor”. Actions live in the footer; the rare or destructive ones behind “…”.

5

Modal language

before / after · every shape

One frame, one footer, one scale. The header is the title plus a close X; the body is the consequence; the footer is the only place with actions. Destructive actions are outlined, never the primary fill. Only two surface types keep their own shell: Settings (a page) and the command palette (its field is the header).
