Health: sidebar report, modal repair

The sidebar holds the report: expandable sections, one row per issue. Clicking an issue opens the repair in a modal in front. Confirm closes it and the sidebar updates — there is nothing to reopen, because the report was never hidden. Two modal shapes to compare.

  • Sidebar is the reportSections expand; the error row is the selection.
  • Click opens the modalOne issue, its context, its choices.
  • Confirm closesThe row leaves the list, Undo in the toast. No next step.

Four groups instead of the audit's categories: what to fix, what is unused, the document structure, the hidden markers. Empty groups do not render. “Anchors to fix” is open by default; the row carries the quote, nothing else.
