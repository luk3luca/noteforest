NoteForest — app icon proposals

Six directions, each at 128 px, then at 64 / 32 / 16 to judge the Dock and the Finder list, and a Dock strip in dark and light. The squircle is the macOS shape (radius ≈ 22 % of the canvas), the paper is the app's page surface, the blue is the default accent.

1 · The card and the branch

A · recommended

Card + branch

A note with a branch leaving it: the gesture the app is built on, one shape, one accent.

At 16 px: a white page with a blue corner. The branch never disappears because it touches the card edge.

64 32 16

B · literal

Two blocks + arrow

The tree itself: a parent and a child. The most faithful and the most explanatory.

At 16 px: two light rectangles and a thin line — the line is the first thing to go. Needs the arrow thickened at small sizes.

64 32 16

C · symmetric

The fork

One block, two children: branching as a shape rather than as a gesture.

At 16 px: three light dots and a blue Y. Balanced, but it says "sitemap" more than "study".

64 32 16

D · the new page

Page with a seam

Two pages touching with the 1 px seam — the Page scroll design, which is the newest thing the app does.

At 16 px: a white column with a line. Clean, but the seam is a detail; it needs the width to exist.

64 32 16

E · neutral

Card + branch, no colour

The same shape with a graphite branch. The icon stops competing with the accent, which the user picks in Appearance.

At 16 px: a page with an L. Sober, but less recognisable in a Dock full of white documents.

64 32 16

F · rejected, for comparison

The tree

The name, literally. It is also the icon of every file manager and half the note apps, and it introduces a green the interface never uses.

At 16 px: three dots and a Y — indistinguishable from a folder tool.

3 · The rules the icon follows

The squircle is the macOS shape (radius ≈ 22 % of the canvas, ~100 px margin), the paper is the app's page surface #fbfbf9, the blue is the default accent. No letters, no rotation, one accent colour, and every thick stroke is drawn twice: the 128 px SVG and a version with a heavier stroke for 32 and 16, because a line that looks right at 128 disappears at 16.

Lab only, no application code, no image files yet. The final icon is generated from the chosen SVG at 1024, then an iconset, then .icns.
