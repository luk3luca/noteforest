Group headers own their divider, the chevron sits on the left, the scroll gutter is reserved, and the needs-repair counter is a dot.

3 · The scroll gutter

The app draws a 10 px classic scrollbar, so a section that starts to overflow loses 10 px of content and everything shifts. With scrollbar-gutter: stable the 10 px are reserved from the start: the thumb is invisible until it is needed, and the content box never changes. Content at 264 is 264 − 8 − 8 − 10 = 238 px.

What this lab is not: it does not restyle the activity bar, the titlebar or the canvas, and it uses an approximation of the default accent. The row and header rules are the subject.
