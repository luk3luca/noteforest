Find bar

⌘F opens a find bar over the current surface. It searches the open note, groups results by chapter and block, and navigates — it is not a query language. Two placements, then the states.

  • Navigation, not searchReturn and ⇧Return move through blocks; Esc puts the selection back.
  • One result per blockThe count is blocks, not every occurrence. The snippet shows the first hit.
  • No layout shiftThe bar floats; Page keeps its zoom, Continuous keeps its flow.
1

Floating bar

recommended · 420 px · top right

A compact bar sits over the surface, 10 px from the top-right corner, with the results panel attached below. Nothing moves: the note keeps its scroll, the page keeps its zoom.

GoodNo reflow, no re-fit, no page jump.
GoodClose to the eye and to the scrollbar side.
CostIt covers the note while open; Esc closes it.

alternative · full width

The bar becomes a strip under the surface toolbar. It never covers the note, but it takes 40 px from the surface: Continuous shifts, and Page re-fits because the available height changed. The results panel hangs below, left-aligned.

Search in the sidebar

264 px · persistent list

The same matching engine, a different presentation: the results live in the sidebar while the note stays uncovered. It is not the quick find bar; it is the document’s search view, the “Search” section already listed as future in the activity bar.

GoodNo overlay; the note stays readable.
GoodFull-height list, persistent while browsing.

Two roles, one engine. The floating bar is the quick find: ⌘F, find the word, jump, esc. The sidebar search is the browsing view: persistent, with the list always visible; it replaces the Outline while active and returns to it on close. I would ship the bar in phase 6 and the sidebar search as the activity bar’s future “Search” section, not mix them in the same batch.

Rules

Case-insensitive, no regex, no replace, no library search. One result per block, snippet from the first hit. The panel keeps the last 50 results and says how many are left; the count is the total.

⌘F open · ↵ next · ⇧↵ previous · ⌘G / ⌘⇧G next/previous · esc close and restore.

Not in v1. Replace, regex, filters, accent folding, results across documents, a document-wide search palette. The bar searches the open note only.

Lab only: no application code. The bar is 40 px high with 28 px controls and the app's 4/8 px radii; the results panel is 420 px wide.
