Structure

Blocks and branches, treated as mass rather than as a diagram. This sheet exists because the structure is the product: a note that grows right and down is the one thing no other notes app has, and an icon that avoids it is an icon of a generic notes app. The rules below are the difference between a mark and a screenshot of the interface — a branch is drawn as a slab, never as a hairline with an arrowhead.

max 4 shapes per icon edge ≥ ⅓ of the block silhouette at 16, not a small picture leaves right or down never enters from below or right arrowheads optional

01

One branch

S01–S14

The sentence the app is built on: a block, a branch, another block. The variants are position and count — right, below, both, high, low, chained. These are the icons that would go on the box.

02

Weight and proportion

S15–S24

The branch is a slab with a variable thickness, and the blocks have variable proportions. This is the family that decides whether the mark survives at 16 px: a thin edge becomes grey mush, a full-height edge becomes a second bar.

03

Fill and form

S25–S34

The same two-block sentence in every material and fill the app owns: rounded, sharp, one accent, all accent, inverted, hollow, half-filled, duotone, accent-only edge, and a block with a shadow that is itself a block.

04

Ends

S35–S40

The most contested question of the previous rounds: arrowheads. Six answers, all entering from the left and pointing right, all with an edge thick enough to survive. Where the head cannot survive at 16 px, the small drawing drops it rather than smudging it.

05

Letters and directions

S41–S46

The composition that happens to be a letter or a direction. These are the closest to a real mark: the structure is there, but the eye reads an L, an N or a colon first.

06

Edge cases

S47–S50

The awkward truths: a port where the branch lands, a child with two parents, two blocks so close they almost merge, and two blocks that do merge. Half of these are probably not shippable; they are here because they test the grammar instead of repeating it.
